# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

Be respectful, constructive, and technically honest. Critique ideas rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or introduce recordings or transcripts without the necessary rights and consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose conduct harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact on the GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical; maintainers must recuse themselves when involved.

This policy is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
